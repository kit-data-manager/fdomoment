Introduction

Welcome to the FAIR DO MoMEnT (FAIR Digital Object Modular Minting & Enablement Toolkit) documentation. This toolkit provides a comprehensive solution for creating FAIR Digital Objects (FAIR DOs) in a modular, intuitive, and effortless way. But why should you care about FAIR DOs?


FAIR Digital Objects adhere to the FAIR principles, ensuring they are:


Findable

FAIR DOs have unique persistent identifiers and rich metadata

Accessible

FAIR DOs are accessible through open, standard protocols

Interoperable

FAIR DOs use standard vocabularies and common data types

Reusable

FAIR DOs have clear licenses and may contain rich provenance information

So no matter if you want to cite your dataset or software as FAIR DO, or if you want to bring them together with publication information, or if you plan to benefit from an evolving FAIR DO ecosystem aiming at fully automated processing of huge amounts of FAIR Digital Objects, starting today with creating your first FAIR DO is a great choice.


FAIR DO MoMEnT comes as a web-based application providing two main interfaces, which can be selected directly on the landing page:


Momentum

Create and publish new FAIR Digital Objects using modular templates.
