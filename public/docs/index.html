Welcome to the FDO MoMEnT (FAIR Digital Object Modular Minting & Enablement Toolkit) documentation. This toolkit provides a comprehensive solution for creating FAIR Digital Objects (FDOs) in a modular, intuitive, and effortless way. But why should you care about FDOs?


FAIR Digital Objects adhere to the FAIR principles, ensuring they are:


Findable

FDOs have unique persistent identifiers and rich metadata

Accessible

FDOs are accessible through open, standard protocols

Interoperable

FDOs use standard vocabularies and common data types

Reusable

FDOs have clear licenses and may contain rich provenance information

So no matter if you want to cite your dataset or software as FDO, or if you want to bring them together with publication information, or if you plan to benefit from an evolving FDO ecosystem aiming at fully automated processing of huge amounts of FAIR Digital Objects, starting today with creating your first FDO is a great choice.


FDO MoMEnT comes as a web-based application providing two main interfaces:


Momentum

Create and publish new FAIR Digital Objects using modular templates.
